# BROWZ – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

BROWZ is a premium beauty, aesthetics and skincare brand operating in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Founded by Michele Barclay in 2016, the business combines specialist brow and lash artistry, advanced aesthetic treatments, skincare and beauty retail.

The homepage redesign focuses on strengthening the brand's premium positioning, improving the presentation of its salon and aesthetic clinic services, and creating clearer pathways for treatment discovery, appointments and product shopping.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Removal of Unrelated and Suspicious Content
- Removed unrelated gambling-style content and suspicious outbound links.
- Refocused the homepage presentation on the business's professional beauty and aesthetics services.
- Improved the consistency of the website's brand messaging.

**Problem addressed:** Unrelated spam and casino content undermined the professional image of the website and could damage visitor trust.

### 2. Clearer Treatment Information
- Reorganised treatment information into clearer service sections.
- Improved the presentation of individual procedures and available treatment options.
- Made service information easier for visitors to understand and explore.

**Problem addressed:** Placeholder-style content and poorly structured treatment information made it difficult for visitors to understand available services and identify relevant options.

### 3. Improved Appointment and Enquiry Experience
- Removed duplicated appointment elements and unfinished template content.
- Improved the visibility and clarity of appointment and enquiry actions.
- Created a more straightforward pathway for visitors interested in requesting a consultation.

**Problem addressed:** Duplicated booking elements, visible editing text and incomplete appointment actions created confusion and unnecessary friction.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Improved the professional appearance of the homepage by removing unrelated content and strengthening brand consistency.
- **Homepage Layout:** Created a clearer content structure for treatments, business information and appointment actions.
- **Treatment Presentation:** Improved the organisation and readability of service information.
- **User Experience:** Reduced confusion caused by duplicated elements and incomplete interactions.
- **Content Consistency:** Refocused homepage messaging around the business's professional services and brand identity.
- **Overall Appearance:** Established a cleaner, more organised and credible homepage experience.

---

**Project Focus:** Professional brand credibility, clearer treatment information and a smoother appointment journey.
